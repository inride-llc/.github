# Security Policy

inride welcomes reports of security vulnerabilities. Protecting our
customers, their data, and our systems is a priority.

## Scope

This policy applies to repositories owned by inride unless a repository
provides its own security policy.

## Supported Versions

Security fixes generally apply to the latest stable release and versions
currently deployed and maintained by inride.

Older releases, archived repositories, and discontinued products do not
receive security updates unless explicitly stated otherwise. If you are
unsure whether a version is supported, please contact us.

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities privately.

- If available, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** in the affected
  GitHub repository.
- Otherwise, email **shaun@inride.com** with the subject
  **Security vulnerability report**.

Do not disclose vulnerability details in public issues, pull requests,
or discussions before we have had an opportunity to investigate.

### What to Include

Please provide:

- The affected repository, product, and version or commit, if known.
- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce the issue.
- A minimal proof of concept, screenshots, or relevant logs.
- Your preferred contact information.

Remove passwords, access tokens, and personal or customer data from
your report. If sensitive evidence is necessary, contact us first to
arrange a secure way to share it.

## What to Expect

We aim to:

- Acknowledge your report within **3 business days**.
- Provide an initial assessment within **10 business days**.
- Provide updates at least every **10 business days** while an accepted
  report remains open.

We prioritize remediation based on severity, exploitability, and
customer impact. Resolution times vary with the complexity of the issue.

If we accept the report, we will coordinate remediation and disclosure
with you. If we decline it, we will explain our assessment where possible.

## Responsible Research and Disclosure

Please use accounts and data you control. Avoid disrupting services,
accessing other users' data, or modifying or deleting information.

If you encounter sensitive data, stop testing and report the finding
promptly. This policy does not grant authorization to test production
systems or third-party services.

Please coordinate public disclosure with us so we can address the
issue and protect affected users. We will discuss attribution with you
before publicly identifying you.
